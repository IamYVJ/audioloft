Studio · multitrack

Layer, arrange and edit many tracks at once

Every file gets its own lane, and lanes play together, so you can put a voice over music or stack instruments. Drag clips in time and between lanes, trim and split them, set each lane's volume, pan, mute and solo, then export the mix, one stem per lane, or every clip.

Why AudioLoft

Built for privacy, tuned for precision

Your browser already has a capable audio engine. AudioLoft puts a careful editor on top of it.

01

Private by design

There's no server to upload to. Files are read, edited and saved inside this tab, and never leave your device.

02

Sample-accurate

Zoom down to individual samples, type exact timestamps, and cuts are crossfaded for a few milliseconds so they never click.

03

The format you need

Audio is decoded at its original sample rate. Export lossless WAV (16, 24 or 32-bit), MP3 up to 320 kbps, or AAC (.m4a) where your browser supports it.

04

Free & open

No accounts, limits or watermarks, and nothing loaded from other sites. Install it as an app and it works offline.

How it works

AudioLoft uses the Web Audio API to decode your file, plain JavaScript to edit the raw samples, and on-device encoders to save the result as WAV, MP3 or AAC, all inside your browser.

MP3 WAV FLAC OGG Opus M4A / AAC WebM AIFF
  1. Drop a file. It's decoded locally, at its own sample rate.
  2. Edit on the waveform. Select, split or reorder, and listen as you go.
  3. Export. WAV, MP3 or AAC (or a ZIP of them) is written straight to your downloads.

Made by Yashvardhan Jain. Format support depends on your browser: anything it can play, AudioLoft can edit.
