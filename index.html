Why AudioLoft

Built for privacy, tuned for precision

Your browser already has a capable audio engine. AudioLoft puts a careful editor on top of it.

01

Private by design

There's no server to upload to. Files are read, edited and saved inside this tab, and never leave your device.

02

Sample-accurate

Zoom down to individual samples, type exact timestamps, and cuts are crossfaded for a few milliseconds so they never click.

03

Lossless output

Audio is decoded at its original sample rate and exported as 16-bit, 24-bit or 32-bit float WAV, with no re-compression.

04

Free & open

No accounts, limits or watermarks, and no third-party libraries. Install it as an app and it works offline.

How it works

AudioLoft uses the Web Audio API to decode your file, plain JavaScript to edit the raw samples, and a hand-written WAV encoder to save the result, all inside your browser.

MP3 WAV FLAC OGG Opus M4A / AAC WebM AIFF
  1. Drop a file. It's decoded locally, at its own sample rate.
  2. Edit on the waveform. Select, split or reorder, and listen as you go.
  3. Export. A WAV (or a ZIP of them) is written straight to your downloads.

Made by Yashvardhan Jain. Format support depends on your browser: anything it can play, AudioLoft can edit.
